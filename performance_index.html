How Scores Are Calculated

The Performance Index evaluates each unit within its peer group (Regions vs Regions, Divisions vs Divisions, CCCs vs CCCs) using a Standardized 0–100 Scale per Metric + Weighted Average.

1. Two-Step Method: Transparent & Future-Proof
Step 1: Every Item on 0–100 Scale
Every KPI is independently scaled from 0 to 100:
• Best in group = 100 / 100
• Worst in group = 0 / 100
• Average = 50 / 100
Step 2: Weighted Average (Total 100)
Each item contributes points proportional to its importance weight %:
Points = Item Score × Weight %
Sum of all points = Overall Index (/100)
2. Parameters and Assigned Weights (Total: 100%)
📉 AT&C Loss %
Weight: 30%
Lower is better. Lowest loss in group gets 100/100
⏱️ NSC Delays (Qtn / WO / Conn)
Weight: 25% Total
Qtn (8%) + WO (8%) + Conn (9%). Lower delay is better
📋 Pending NSC Backlog
Weight: 25%
Fewer pending applications = higher score
💳 Pending Master Cards (>3 Months)
Weight: 20%
Unbilled meters installed >90 days ago. Fewer pending = higher score.
3. Tabular Calculation Example
Suppose in a group, Best Loss is 14.0% and Worst is 26.0% (Span = 12.0%).
If an office has 20.0% loss:
Step 1 (100 Scale): 100 × (26% − 20%) ÷ (26% − 14%) = 100 × (6 ÷ 12) = 50.0 / 100
Step 2 (Weight 30%): 50.0 × 30% = 15.00 Points Contributed
💡 Adding more parameters in the future: Every new item will simply be scored 0–100, and assigned an appropriate weight %, keeping the system 100% modular, fair, and scalable!
