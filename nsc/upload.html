NSC Upload

How to upload
  1. Select the data date (as-on date of this NSC file).
  2. Choose the pending NSC Excel file.
  3. Click Upload & Publish and wait until it finishes.
  4. Open the NSC dashboard to see the updated list.

Only Working and Accepted applications are published. Withheld rows are skipped automatically.

Admin: cleaned Working/Accepted rows publish to the live NSC store (local backup when available). Dashboards read via DataHub cache. Grant rights under Admin → User Management → NSC Raw Upload.

Use the as-on date of this Excel. It appears as “Updated on” on the dashboard.
