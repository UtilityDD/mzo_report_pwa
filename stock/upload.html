Stock upload

Publish a new SAP stock extract to the Stock page

  1. Set the data date (as-on date of the SAP extract).
  2. Choose the SAP stock Excel (Sheet1 with Material and Material Group).
  3. Upload & publish, keep this tab open, then check the Stock page.

Admins and users with Stock upload can publish. The file is processed in this browser.
