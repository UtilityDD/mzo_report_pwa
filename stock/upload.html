Replace the figures everyone sees on the Stock page.

How to publish
  1. Set the data date — the as-on date of this SAP extract.
  2. Choose the SAP stock Excel. Sheet1 must have Material and Material Group.
  3. Click Upload & Publish and wait until it finishes. Do not close the tab.
  4. Open Stock to confirm the new as-on date and quantities.

Only admin or users with Stock upload = Yes can publish. The file is cleaned in this browser; the live dump is stored for all offices.
