Stock Raw Excel Upload

How to upload
  1. Select the data / report date (as-on date). This is shown on the Stock dashboard header.
  2. Choose the SAP stock Excel (.xlsx / .xlsb / .csv). Use Sheet1 for stock rows (Material + Material Group required).
  3. Local / Central category is fixed in the app by material code (not in the Excel).
  4. Click Upload & Publish and wait for large files.
  5. Open Stock and refresh if needed.

Admin: publishes to Supabase stock_snapshot (+ local backup). Sheet remains fallback until removed.
